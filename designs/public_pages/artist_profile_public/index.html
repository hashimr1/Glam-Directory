About the Artist

Hi, I'm Sarah! With over 8 years of experience in the beauty industry, I specialize in creating timeless, radiant looks for brides and editorial campaigns. My philosophy is to enhance natural beauty rather than mask it. I believe every face tells a story, and my job is to help you tell yours with confidence and grace.

Based in New York City, I have had the privilege of working with hundreds of brides and have been featured in top wedding publications. I use only premium, long-lasting products to ensure you look flawless from the first photo to the last dance. When I'm not with a brush in hand, I'm constantly researching the latest trends and techniques to bring the best to my clients.
